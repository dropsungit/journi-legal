Journi

Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

The short version

  • We only use your information to run your Journi account and the app's features.
  • We don't sell your information, rent it, or share it with advertisers. There are no ads and no tracking.
  • Your games, logs, and posts can be seen by other Journi users. Your email address and password can't.
  • You can export everything you've logged, and delete your account, from Settings in the app.

What we collect

What

Why

Email address and password

To create your account and sign you in. Passwords are stored scrambled (hashed), so we can't read them.

Username, profile colors, Top Four, Featured Physicals, Game of the Year picks

To show your profile.

What you log: play logs, play times, ratings, reviews, backlog, collection, Want to Get, and lists

Posts, comments, likes, reshares, follows

To run the Home feed and profiles.

Blocks and reports

To hide people you've blocked and to review reported content.

Feedback and bug reports you send

To fix problems and improve Journi.

Import history

So you can see what an import added, in Settings › Import Logs.

We don't collect your location, contacts, advertising ID, or browsing activity, and we don't use analytics or tracking tools.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Imported files. HowLongToBeat exports and spreadsheets are read on your phone. Only the game titles are sent to look up the matching games; the file itself is never uploaded.
  • Photos and videos you attach to a post currently stay on your phone and aren't uploaded.
  • Your data copy. Journi keeps a copy of your logged games on your iPhone (see Settings › Export My Data). It's included in your iPhone's iCloud Backup, which is controlled by you and Apple, not by us.

Who can see what

  • Other Journi users can see your username, profile, posts, comments, reviews, play logs, lists, and collection. Want to Get is visible unless you turn it off in Settings.
  • Nobody else using the app can see your email address, password, blocks, reports, feedback, or import history.
  • When you block someone, you won't see their posts or comments.

We don't sell your information

We don't sell, rent, or trade your personal information, and we don't share it with advertisers or data brokers. We don't use it for advertising of any kind.

Services we use to run Journi

These companies process data only to provide their service to us, and aren't allowed to use it for anything else:

  • Supabase stores your account and everything you log or post.
  • Cloudflare runs the small server that looks up game information.
  • IGDB (owned by Twitch) provides game details, covers, and play times. It receives the game names you search for or import, but not your account or email.
  • GitHub stores our encrypted nightly backups of the database.
  • Apple delivers the app (App Store and TestFlight) and, if you use it, iCloud Backup.

We'll also share information if the law requires it, or to protect people from harm.

How long we keep it

  • We keep your information while you have an account.
  • When you delete your account (Settings › Delete Account), your account and everything connected to it are deleted right away.
  • Copies may remain in our encrypted backups for up to 30 days, after which they're gone too.

Your choices

  • See and download your data: Settings › Export My Data.
  • Change it: edit or delete any log, review, post, comment, list, or collection entry in the app.
  • Delete your account: Settings › Delete Account.
  • Anything else (including a full copy of your account information): email us at the address below.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections, every table in our database is locked to its owner or to signed-in users as described above, and our backups are encrypted. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to keep your information safe.

Children

Journi isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you think a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll also let you know in the app.
